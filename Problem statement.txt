Problem Statement

Organizations must follow cybersecurity standards such as NIST and ISO to protect their digital assets.
However, writing, updating, and maintaining cybersecurity policies and procedures manually is:

Time-consuming

Complex and error-prone

Difficult to keep updated with frequently changing standards

Not tailored properly to each organization’s size, infrastructure, and risk profile

Because of this, many organizations end up with outdated, incomplete, or ineffective security policies, which increases the risk of cyberattacks and non-compliance.

👉 Therefore, there is a need for an automated, intelligent system that can generate accurate, up-to-date, and organization-specific cybersecurity policies efficiently.


one line

This project automates the generation of cybersecurity policies using AI, ensuring they are accurate, compliant with NIST/ISO standards, and customized to an organization’s environment.
