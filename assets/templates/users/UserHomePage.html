What is a Security Policy?

A cybersecurity policy defines technical and non-technical assets an organization must protect. It establishes regulations, roles, and implementation procedures.

Policies include risk registers to promote accountability and awareness of potential threats.

What is a Cybersecurity Risk?

Cybersecurity risk is the probability of an event that may compromise assets such as data, systems, reputation, or identity.

Risks are assessed based on likelihood and impact, including latent vulnerabilities.

Why Do We Need a Security Policy?

Security policies provide a structured framework to protect confidential data, intellectual property, and infrastructure.

They reduce unauthorized access, data breaches, and operational disruptions.
