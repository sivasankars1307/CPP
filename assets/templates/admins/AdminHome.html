Organizational Dataset

The organizational environment is a critical parameter in developing cybersecurity policies. For the generated policy to be able to address most of the security vulnerabilities and to be able to generate the necessary effective guidelines, it must match the organization's specific infrastructure, objectives, and risk register. For this reason, these security policies should be dynamically dependent on the organization's infrastructure.

What is a security policy


A cybersecurity policy lists the technical and nontechnical assets an organization should safeguard. It also lists the possible threats to resources and provides regulations and guidelines to protect both them and the organization. Usually, a security policy is written in parallel to a procedure of execution, to ensure that it can be implementable and efficient. The policy and procedure highlight rules and assign roles for implementation and monitoring. A list of responsibilities should also be set and a risk register must be provided to encourage responsible parties to implement the policy and understand the possible harm of different security breaches.

What is a cybersecurity risk


As described by various regulation institutes, a security risk is the likelihood of occurrence of a specific scenario that might cause loss or compromise of an organization's assets. Risk is also measured by the possible impact of the scenario on the specified assets. Assets can be data, systems, or even reputation and identity. In some other cases, security risks can be defined by a list of vulnerabilities that have not yet been shown but have the probability of occurrence at specific times which may cause harm.

Why do we need a Security Policy


CyberSecurity policies serve as an effective framework for establishing and maintaining information security guidelines within an organization. These guidelines ensure the protection of assets including confidential data, intellectual ownership, physical equipment, and more. Policies reduce the risk of unauthorized access to these assets that can cause data theft or harm. Moreover, cybersecurity risks are hard to measure and be assessed. Security policies help identify and assess these cybersecurity risks, by implementing measures that help organizations to mitigate these risks effectively.
